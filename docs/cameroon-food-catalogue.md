# Cameroon food catalogue

This batch adds 12 offline entries. The source field is displayed on the portion screen with an estimate notice. The 100 g baseline is the edible prepared dish. Use grams to log the amount served. Values can vary substantially with oil, water, ingredient ratios and preparation. Existing entries and historical records are preserved.

- Ponka et al. (2016), Table 2. Laboratory measurements of sampled dishes. Used for Ekomba and Tenue militaire. [Open-access paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5011377/)
- Sharma et al. (2007), Tables I–IV. Calculated averages from weighed recipes in Yaoundé and rural Central Cameroon. Used for the remaining ten entries. [Paper and DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/09637480701288454)

The bilingual and local aliases are search labels, added by MboaFit. Recipe values describe source preparations. The app assigns no goal or health rating to these new entries. For future batches, review the [Congo Basin Food Composition Table](https://www.cifor-icraf.org/knowledge/publication/8936/) and prioritize gaps that testers report in each region. Require a source, preparation and edible-weight basis for each addition. Use the existing custom food feature for unlisted items.
